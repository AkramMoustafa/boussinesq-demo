Boussinesq Vorticity Predictor

A Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) trained on the 2D Boussinesq system. Given initial vorticity and temperature fields, it predicts the vorticity at t = 100 on a 128×128 periodic domain [0, 2π]².

Model FNO
Resolution 128×128
Epochs 1200
Norm H2-normalized ICs
Domain [0, 2π]²

Architecture

FNO — 4 layers, 64 modes, 64 hidden channels

Training data

10,000 trajectories, ν = 1, T = 100

Initial condition norm

‖ω⊂0;‖H² + ‖θ⊂0;‖H² = 0.2

Training loss

H1 loss, StepLR scheduler
